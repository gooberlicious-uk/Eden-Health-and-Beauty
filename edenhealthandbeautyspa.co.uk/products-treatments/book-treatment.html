Our Cancellation Policy

We ask for 24 hours notice with a cancellation to allow us chance to rebook the appointment slot. There will be a 50% charge of the total appointment cost if we have less than 24 hours notice and are unable to fill the gap.

With the present Covid -19 situation, missed appointments or appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice that are unable to be refilled, will be charged at 100%.
